Guide

How LÂNEA Books works — the full workflow in one page.

The stock model

Everything you buy (Purchases) and your initial inventory (Catalog) enters the Warehouse. From the Stocks tab you transfer stock to a point of sale when you supply it. A sale in the Daily report consumes that POS's stock; a gift consumes the warehouse. A submitted inventory count corrects the counted location to reality. At all times: Global stock = Warehouse + all points of sale, and every product's value is carried at its average cost (invoice discounts and shipping included).

Daily routine

Open Daily report → pick the day on the calendar (grey = submitted, amber = unfinished) → add each sale (POS → product → qty → price auto-fills → Enter), plus gifts and expenses → check the live totals → Submit daily report once. Corrections: "Edit" on a saved entry pulls it back into the report.

When goods move

New supplier delivery → Purchases tab (add lines, or send the PDF invoice to Claude to extend the import). Supplying a boutique or exhibition → Stocks → "Send to POS"; leftovers → "Return to warehouse".

Counting & reconciling

Inventory tab → open a fiche d'inventaire for one location → type the actual counted quantities (drafts pause and resume) → Submit → stock snaps to reality and the écart (units and €) stays in the inventory list forever.

Reading the business

Dashboard: revenue, COGS, gross, expenses, net, stock value, low stock — filter by period, POS or product. Stocks: the per-product statement (initial + purchased − sold − gifted ± adjustments = stock) and the warehouse/POS matrix. Sales & prices: each POS's performance and its price list (bulk edit + Save prices). Catalog: products by category. Everything exports to CSV; the dashboard prints to PDF.

What saves when

Daily reports and inventory sheets save when you Submit (drafts survive closing the browser). Prices save with the "Save prices" button. Everything else (products, purchases, transfers) saves instantly with a ✓ confirmation. Signed in = every save syncs to the cloud and appears on all your devices. Settings keeps a JSON backup as a safety net.
